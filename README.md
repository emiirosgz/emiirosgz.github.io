## 🚀 About Me
I am a professional hybrid blending graphic design, UI/UX, and frontend development. Currently pursuing a degree in Software Development Engineering, I have over 9 years of experience creating and collaborating on digital projects. My technical focus lies in building robust, lightweight web applications utilizing **Astro, Tailwind CSS, and React**, prioritizing clean code logic over heavy visual builders.

With a background as a digital marketing analyst, I bring a strategic perspective to my code, actively integrating SEO best practices, structured JSON-LD architectures, and optimized layouts. I distinguish myself through adaptability, attention to detail, and a constant drive to master new technologies.
